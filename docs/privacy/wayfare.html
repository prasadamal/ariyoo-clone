Wayfare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026
Contact: privacy@ariyoo.app

Wayfare ("we", "the app") is the offline-first travel journal product in the Ariyoo family of apps (bundle ID com.wayfare.app).

What we collect

  • Account information: email and profile fields when you register (Supabase Auth).
  • Trip and journal data: trip titles, destinations, journal text, moods, places, budgets, packing lists, and GPS route points you record.
  • Photos: images you attach to journal entries (on device; in cloud when you sync).
  • Location: latitude/longitude while you actively record a route on a trip.

How we use data

  • Offline journaling and optional cloud backup/sync
  • Authentication and associating data with your account

We do not sell your personal information.

Storage and security

Local data is stored in on-device SQLite. Cloud data is stored in Supabase with row-level security. Photos use private storage buckets with authenticated access.

Your choices

You may use offline-only mode without an account. Sign out and contact us to request account deletion.

Children

Wayfare is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
