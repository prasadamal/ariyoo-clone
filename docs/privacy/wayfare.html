Wayfare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026
Contact: privacy@ariyoo.app

Wayfare ("we", "the app", bundle ID com.wayfare.app) is a travel journal and traveller community in the Ariyoo family of apps. Your journal is private by default: nothing you write is shown to anyone unless you post it, share a link, or switch on a community feature.

1. Using Wayfare without an account

You can journal without signing up. Trips, entries, photos, voice notes, plans, packing lists, expenses and GPS routes then stay only in your phone’s storage and we receive none of it. Weather and currency lookups (section 6) still reach their providers without identifying you.

3. Location in detail

  • Route recording uses precise location, including in the background, only between “Start recording” and “Stop recording”. Android shows a persistent notification while it runs.
  • Entry geotags and weather read your location once when you save a moment with “Add location” switched on.
  • Nearby and live location are off until you turn them on and switch off automatically.
  • Verified tiers are computed on our server from your own uploaded route points (distance and altitude only).

4. Links you share

A read-only trip link shows that trip’s title, destination, dates, journal text, moods, places, weather, a simplified route shape and your display name to anyone with the link, until you turn it off. Photos and voice notes are not included. A live-location link shows your name, its label and your latest position until it expires or you stop it.

5. How we use data

  • To provide journaling, backup and sync across your devices.
  • To run community features you choose to use: feeds, profiles, chats, the travel board, tips, trip matching and nearby.
  • To send notifications you enabled (messages, follows, join requests, trip matches).
  • To keep the community safe: on-device filtering of abusive words and scam phrasing, reports and blocks, automatic hiding of content reported by several people, and review by our team.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for advertising.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database, file storage and realtime chat hosting.
  • Expo — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • Open-Meteo — weather and forecasts; receives coordinates only.
  • ExchangeRate-API / Frankfurter (ECB) — currency rates; receive no personal data.
  • Google Maps (in-app maps) and OpenStreetMap (web viewers) — map tiles for the area being displayed.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Journal and community data are kept until you delete them or your account.
  • Nearby positions expire within 24 hours; live-location links within 72 hours.
  • Delete your account in the app (You → Settings & privacy → Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile, cloud journals, uploaded files, posts, comments, messages, board posts, tips and push tokens. Residual copies may remain in encrypted infrastructure backups for up to 30 days. Without the app, request deletion as described on Delete your Wayfare account.
  • Reports about content are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe.

8. Your choices

  • Control who can find you (Discoverable) and who can message you (everyone, people you follow, nobody).
  • Post to everyone or followers only; delete posts, comments and messages you sent at any time.
  • Block or report any traveller or content.
  • Export a trip as PDF, GPX or GeoJSON.
  • Ask us for a copy or correction of your data: privacy@ariyoo.app.

9. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Cloud data is protected with row-level security so each traveller can only access what they are allowed to see; journal media and chat photos are in private storage. Profile photos and post photos are stored as public files at unguessable addresses so feeds load quickly; anyone who obtains such an address can view that image. An optional biometric lock protects the app on your phone.

10. Age

Wayfare accounts and community features are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

11. Changes

We will update the effective date above and notify you in the app about material changes.
