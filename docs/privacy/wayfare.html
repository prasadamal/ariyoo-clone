Wayfare Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026
Who we are: Wayfare (app ID com.wayfare.app) is a travel journal made by Amal Prasad, India ("we", "us"). For your data we are the Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
Contact and Grievance Officer: Amal Prasad, amalprasadappu@gmail.com (section 11).

In short. Your journal is private. Without an account it never leaves your phone. With an account it is backed up to our database in Mumbai, India, so you can restore it and share journeys with people you choose. We don't show ads, sell data, or use third-party analytics or tracking. You can delete your account and its data in the app at any time.

1. Using Wayfare without an account

You can journal without signing up. Journeys, moments, photos, voice notes, plans, packing lists, expenses and routes then stay only on your phone, and we receive none of them. The few online lookups the app makes (weather, currency rates and map tiles, section 6) reach those providers without your name or account.

3. Permissions and what happens on your phone

  • Location is used when you add a location to a moment, record a journey's route (only between "Start recording" and "Stop recording", with a notification shown the whole time), or start a live-location link. Wayfare never tracks you otherwise and does not use background location.
  • Camera and photos: you take a photo or pick photos with the phone's photo picker. Wayfare sees only the photos you choose.
  • Microphone is used only while you record a voice note.
  • Written-out voice notes (off by default, Settings): your phone's speech-recognition service turns your voice into words. On Android this is usually Google's service, which may process the audio under Google's own terms. The words are saved in your moment.
  • Notifications are used for reminders you turn on (daily diary, packing) and journey updates.

4. Why we use your data

We use it only to run Wayfare: to keep your journal, back it up and restore it, let you share journeys and links you choose, send notifications you turned on, keep accounts secure and stop misuse, fix problems, and improve the app. We rely on your consent, given when you create an account or turn a feature on; you can withdraw it at any time by turning the feature off or deleting your account. We may also process data where the law requires it. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Where your data is stored

Account and journal data is stored with Supabase in its Mumbai (India) region. Some service providers in section 6 handle small amounts of data (such as your IP address or the map area you view) on servers outside India. We only use providers that protect data to standards similar to ours, and we follow any restrictions the Government of India places on transfers.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase: sign-in, database and file storage (Mumbai, India).
  • Expo: delivers push notifications to your phone.
  • Open-Meteo: weather and forecasts; receives coordinates only.
  • Frankfurter / ExchangeRate-API: currency rates; receive no personal data.
  • OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap: map tiles for the area shown on a map; receives that area and your IP address.
  • Render: hosts the web pages for story links, live-location links and this policy.
  • Google (Android): your phone's speech recognition, if you turn on written-out voice notes; and Android's own backup of app data to your Google account, if you have device backup turned on.

7. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • Journal and profile data: until you delete it or your account.
  • Live-location links end within 72 hours; invite links within 7 days.
  • Usage counts and crash reports: only as long as needed to fix problems and measure use.
  • Delete your account in the app: You → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your profile, cloud journals, uploaded photos and voice notes, links and push tokens straight away. Any copies left in our providers' systems (such as logs) expire within 30 days. Feedback you sent is kept without your account attached. Without the app, see Delete your Wayfare account.
  • Journals saved only on your phone stay there until you remove them (Settings → Remove journals from this phone) or uninstall the app.

8. Your rights

Under Indian law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it (most of it you can change in the app);
  • erase it (you can delete your account in the app);
  • withdraw your consent (turn the feature off, or delete your account);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to;
  • resolve a complaint (section 11).

Email us from the address on your account. We answer within 7 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules let each person reach only their own journals and those shared with them. Photos and voice notes are kept in private storage; a story link makes a journey's photos viewable only while that link is on. You can lock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN. Your phone keeps its own full copy of your journal; on our current hosting plan the cloud database has no separate point-in-time backups, so export a journey as a PDF if you can't risk losing it. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

10. Age

Wayfare accounts are for people aged 18 and over; you confirm this when you sign up. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we will delete it.
