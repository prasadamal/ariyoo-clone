Wayfare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026
Contact: privacy@ariyoo.app

Wayfare is a private travel journal (bundle ID com.wayfare.app). Your journal lives on your device first. If you create an account, it is also backed up to your private cloud space so you can restore it on a new phone.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it goes
Email address and display name (account)Sign-in, password resetSupabase Auth
Trips, journal entries, moods, place names, budgets, packing listsThe journal itself, backup, sync between your devicesOn device; Supabase database if you have an account

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, do not track you across apps, and do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Location

Wayfare asks for location only while you use the app (“While Using”). Route recording continues in the background only after you start it, and shows a persistent notification (Android) or status indicator (iOS) until you stop it. You can stop at any time.

Sharing

Your journal is private by default. If you choose “Share read-only link” for a trip, anyone who has that link can read the trip title, destination, dates, notes and journal entry text (including place, mood and weather). Photos, voice notes, route, budget and companions are not included. You can turn the link off at any time, and it stops working immediately.

Service providers

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage (region: Mumbai, India). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by row-level security so each account can only read its own rows, and photos/voice notes are stored in a private bucket.
  • Open-Meteo — weather lookup (coordinates only).
  • Google Maps (Android) / Apple Maps (iOS) — map display for your recorded route.

Security

Sign-in tokens are kept in the device’s secure keychain/keystore. Optional biometric lock protects the app on your device; biometric data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Retention and deletion

  • Deleting a trip, entry, expense or photo in the app removes it from the cloud the next time the app syncs.
  • Delete account: Profile → Delete account permanently erases your account, cloud journal and files. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • Short-lived infrastructure backups may persist for a limited period after deletion and are then overwritten.
  • Without an account, everything stays on your device; uninstalling the app removes it.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (PDF/GeoJSON from within the app) and delete your data. Under India’s DPDP Act, the GDPR/UK GDPR and similar laws you may also withdraw consent, object to processing, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Email privacy@ariyoo.app and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Wayfare is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
