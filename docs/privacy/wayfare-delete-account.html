Delete your Wayfare account

App: Wayfare (com.wayfare.app)

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Wayfare and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all cloud data are erased immediately.

Without the app

Email privacy@ariyoo.app from the address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my Wayfare account”. We will verify the request and complete deletion within 30 days (usually within 3 days).

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, display name, sign-in credentials)
  • All trips, journal entries, expenses, packing lists and GPS route points
  • All photos and voice notes in cloud storage
  • Any shared read-only links (they stop working)

Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or choose “Delete account” (which also clears the on-device copy). Infrastructure backups may keep encrypted copies for a short period before being overwritten.
