Delete your Wayfare account

Wayfare (com.wayfare.app) lets you delete your account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Wayfare and sign in.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and tap Delete forever.

Your account and its cloud data are deleted straight away.

No longer have the app?

Email privacy@ariyoo.app from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Wayfare account”. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address before we act on it.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, photo and travel stats.
  • Cloud copies of your journeys: moments, photos, voice notes, plans, packing lists, expenses and GPS routes.
  • Posts, comments, likes, follows, travel-board posts and join requests, destination tips, chat messages and chat photos.
  • Share links and live-location links, which stop working.
  • Push notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Encrypted infrastructure backups can hold residual copies for up to 30 days before they expire.
  • Reports other people filed about content are kept as long as needed to keep the community safe, without your account attached.
  • Journeys saved only on your phone stay there until you remove them (Settings & privacy → Remove journals from this phone) or uninstall the app.
