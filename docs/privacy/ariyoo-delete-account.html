Delete your Ariyoo account

Ariyoo (com.ariyoo.mobile) lets you delete your account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Ariyoo and sign in.
  2. Tap the Profile tab and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password and tap Delete forever.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away.

No longer have the app, or forgot your password?

Email amalprasadappu@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Ariyoo account”. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address before we act on it.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, username and password hash.
  • Your topics and expert setting.
  • Every conversation you took part in, including the other person's messages in it, and all photos sent in those conversations.
  • Your block list, reports you filed, and your notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Infrastructure backups can hold residual copies for up to 30 days before they expire.
  • Reports other people filed about you are kept, without your name or email, as long as needed to keep the service safe.
