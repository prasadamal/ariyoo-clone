Trust & Safety

Strangers should be able to help each other safely.

What our Ariyoo app does today to protect the people who use it. We describe what is in the product, and nothing we have not built. Wayfare and ReelMates also include reporting, blocking and in-app account deletion.

Safety in every conversation

  • Report and block. Anyone can report a conversation or block a person at any time. Blocking closes the conversation for both sides.
  • Automatic filtering. Messages containing abusive or dangerous content are stopped before they are sent.
  • Moderation. Reports reach our moderators, who can review a report and suspend an account. A suspended account cannot sign in.
  • Terms acceptance. Everyone accepts the Terms of Use and community rules before creating an account.

Privacy and data

  • No tracking. The Ariyoo app contains no advertising and no analytics software, and does not collect your location, contacts or advertising identifier.
  • Private photos. A photo in a conversation can be opened only by the two people in it.
  • Your data, your call. You can delete your account in the app. Your conversations, including photos, are removed.
  • Not for sale. We do not sell personal information.

Security

  • All connections use HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text.
  • Sign-in and public forms are rate-limited to slow down automated abuse.
  • Each conversation is visible only to its two participants.

Accountability for experts

  • Only the person who asked can close a question, and say whether the answer helped.
  • An expert's public record is the count of questions they saw through and how many helped.
  • Experts can be blocked, reported and suspended like anyone else.

Report a concern

In the app, use the menu in a conversation to report or block. To report a safety or security concern, or to tell us about a vulnerability, write to Ariyoo@test.com. Please include enough detail for us to find the issue, and do not include passwords.

Read our full Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

No system is perfectly secure. We are a young company and we will keep improving; if we get something wrong, we want to hear about it.
