Our apps

Three apps, one standard of care.

Local answers

Ask a local. Get a real answer.

Ask about any place and get matched with a local expert who knows it first-hand, in a private conversation built for safety.

  • Questions reach local experts by topic, or wait in an open queue for any expert
  • Private chat with photos, visible only to the two people in it
  • Askers mark questions answered and say whether they helped
  • A personal library to search past answers and save the best ones
  • Report, block and moderation built into every conversation
More about Ariyoo

Travel journal

Every journey, and every day in between.

A travel journal that works offline: write, add photos and voice notes, plan and budget trips, and keep a private everyday journal between journeys.

  • Journal moments offline with photos, voice notes, place and weather
  • A private everyday journal, with "On this day" memories from past years
  • Plan days, track a budget, split costs and pack from past trip lists
  • Safety tools: SOS, live location sharing and check-in reminders
  • Find travellers heading the same way, when you choose to
More about Wayfare

Film taste

Your taste in films, shared.

Rate today's films in a few swipes, keep a film diary and a watchlist, and find the people whose taste in films matches yours.

  • A daily set of films to rate in seconds
  • The People's Chart: what members love, by collection
  • A film diary with dates, half-star ratings and notes
  • A watchlist for everything you mean to see
  • Compare taste with friends and see how closely you match

How we build

The same commitments in every app.

Safety tools built in

Every app lets people report and block. Problems reach a person who can act on them, not a dead inbox.

Your data stays yours

Every app lets you delete your account from inside the app. No hunting through settings pages or sending emails.

Made for real life

Answers from people who live there, a journal that works without signal, film picks that take seconds. Useful first, clever second.
