=============================

For the company, its website and the other apps, see ../../PROMPT_ME.txt (repo root).

1. THE IDEA
-----------
"Ariyo? Ask someone who knows." (Ariyo = "do you know?" in Malayalam.)
An expert Q&A app for travel and local life: a traveller asks a question ("best sadya in Kochi?",
"Vypin ferry timings?"), it goes to a local expert with matching topics (#kochi, #kerala food),
and they answer in a chat. Experts are locals: homestay owners, trek leaders, guides, bloggers,
long-time residents who already answer DMs for free.
It is a two-sided marketplace: hardest shape of all the founder's apps (needs experts before
askers). Analysis advice was "park it"; the founder chose a SMALL launch to grow slowly.


2. THE END PRODUCT
------------------
A trusted "ask a local" app, starting in two places (Kerala and Goa), with fast answers
(target: median first answer under 60 minutes), public expert profiles experts share on their
own channels, and a growing searchable library of answers that is useful even without asking.


3. FEATURES THAT EXIST TODAY
----------------------------
- Sign-up/sign-in with 18+ and Terms/Privacy acceptance; Ask / Ask-and-answer roles.
- Ask a question (tappable example questions); matching to the least-busy expert with the
  topic, else an open queue any expert can pick up (bug where a question went to the asker or
  one expert got everything — fixed).
- Chat with photos; "Mark as answered" + "did it help?" thank-you; expert record
  ("12 answers · 9 found helpful").
- Experts add/remove their topics in the app.
- Personal answer library: search everything you asked/answered; save (bookmark) answers.
- Report and block from any conversation; blocked list; account deletion (with password);
  change password; notifications for new questions/replies (no message text).
- Moderation: admin console at https://ariyoo-api-cboa.onrender.com/admin-console (reports with
  the reported messages, resolve/dismiss, suspend/delete, find member by email); unlock with
  ADMIN_API_KEY from Render → Environment. services/api/MODERATION.md has the commands.
  Reports must be handled within 24 hours (store rule).
- Redesign (19 Nov): identity "a trusted local friend" — warm paper, deep ink, Kerala-green
  accent, saffron only for experts; light and dark; WCAG AA contrast.
- Upgraded from Expo 51 to 57 (Play would not accept 51).


4. ON HOLD / NOT BUILT
----------------------
- Password reset (needs an email provider + domain; support handles resets by hand for now).
- Photo storage: Render free has no disk, photos are stored in the Neon database for now; move
  to file storage once the database passes ~500 MB.
- Ideas: searchable public answer library by place, trip collections of saved answers, trip
  planner from answers, place notes for experts, offline saved answers, pre-trip checklist,
  payments for experts, founding-expert badge and shareable expert profile link.
- Web client (apps/web) only got the terms checkbox, not report/block/deletion (mobile-first).


5. TECHNICAL MAP
----------------
- API: Node/Express in services/api, live on Render free tier: https://ariyoo-api-cboa.onrender.com
  (sleeps after 15 min idle). Database: Neon free Postgres (never pauses, 1 GB).
  Migrations applied live include 20261117000000_launch_safety and the saved-answers table.
- Mobile: Expo SDK 57 in apps/mobile; `npm run preflight` blocks a store build without the real
  server address. Expo account prasadaamals-team, project "ariyoo".
- Tests: server unit tests + a full end-to-end flow on a real Postgres; app unit tests + typecheck.
- Store paperwork: apps/mobile/store/ (privacy, terms, deletion pages, form answers,
  SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md, EAS_SECRETS.md).

7. MARKETING (from the 19 Nov plan)
-----------------------------------
- To experts: "You already answer travel questions in your DMs. Do it in one place, get a public
  profile, and build a following."  To askers: "Skip 40 blog tabs. Ask someone who lives there."
- Cold start: experts first; seed 3-5 good questions per topic; then open to askers via r/Kerala,
  r/goa and trip-planning threads; homestay/hostel QR cards "Ask a local about this place".
- Launch: weeks 1-8 recruit experts (closed testing), week 9 open in Kerala and Goa only, add a
  new place only when the first two answer 80% of questions within an hour.
- Targets (90 days): 50 active experts, 300 questions answered, 40% of askers ask again in 30 days.
