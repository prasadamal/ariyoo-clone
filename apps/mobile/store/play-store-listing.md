## Short description (80 chars max)

## Full description
Ariyoo connects you with knowledgeable locals and travel experts when you need trustworthy answers—not generic search results.

**For seekers**
- Ask questions with hashtags to match the right expert
- Chat in real time with matched solvers
- Share photos for context

**For experts (solvers)**
- Enable Guru mode in your profile
- Receive questions matched to your tags
- Help travelers and locals with your expertise

Whether you're planning a trip to Goa, exploring Kerala, or navigating a new city, Ariyoo helps you get answers from people who've been there.
