# Ariyoo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026  
**Contact:** amalprasadappu@gmail.com

Ariyoo ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ariyoo mobile application, which connects seekers with expert solvers for travel and local Q&A.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** email address, display name, and password (stored hashed on our servers).
- **Profile data:** optional username, seeker/solver preferences, and interest tags.
- **Messages and content:** questions, chat messages, and images you choose to share in sessions.
- **Device data:** standard mobile diagnostics (crash logs, OS version) when you opt in to analytics.

We do not sell personal information.

## How we use information

- Provide matching between seekers and solvers
- Deliver chat and notifications
- Maintain security and prevent abuse
- Improve app reliability

## Data retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion by emailing amalprasadappu@gmail.com.

## Third parties

We use infrastructure providers for hosting and may use Apple/Google distribution services. Message content is processed on servers you configure (`EXPO_PUBLIC_API_URL`).

## Children's privacy

Ariyoo is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version in the app store listing and repository.

## Your rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data.
