## Description
Ariyoo is your pocket expert network for travel and local discovery.

Ask a question, add hashtags like #goa or #food, and get matched with a solver who knows the topic. Continue the conversation in a private chat, share photos, and build confidence before your trip.

Experts can turn on Solver mode to receive matched questions and help the community.

**Highlights**
- Real-time chat with Socket-powered messaging
- Tag-based expert matching
- Secure sign-in with encrypted token storage on device
- Dark, distraction-free interface
