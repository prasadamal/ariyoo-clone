## Description
Wayfare helps you capture the trips that matter: Himalayan treks, road trips and city breaks — with tools that work without signal.

• Journal with mood, place and optional local weather
• Attach photos and record voice notes
• Per-trip budget (₹) and packing lists — add your own items
• Record your GPS route on a map; export as GeoJSON
• Export your whole journal as a PDF
• Curated trek guides: permits, seasons, connectivity
• Optional app lock with Face ID or passcode
• Share a read-only link to a trip — turn it off any time
• Use it without an account, or sign in to back up and sync
• Delete your account and all cloud data from inside the app

No ads. No tracking.
